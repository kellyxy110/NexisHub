# NRI AI Visibility Index Pilot Invitation

## Internal draft

This is an invitation template for founder and research-lead review. It must not be sent until the study purpose, data basis, opt-out process, and publication treatment are approved.

## Subject

Invitation to participate in the Nexis Research Institute AI Visibility Index pilot

## Message

Hello [name],

Nexis Research Institute is conducting a small pilot study on how public websites are discovered, interpreted, and represented in AI-assisted information systems.

We are inviting a limited number of organisations from different sectors to help test the research framework. The pilot is intended to improve the definitions and methods. It is not a public ranking and does not produce a guaranteed visibility, citation, or business-performance score.

If you agree to participate, the study team will review selected public pages from your website and run a declared set of information queries. We will record the collection date, the pages reviewed, the method used, and the observed results. We will not request passwords or private content.

Before participation, we will provide:

- the study protocol
- the page and query selection rules
- the data we intend to collect
- the retention period
- the opt-out and correction process
- the proposed publication treatment

You may decline, withdraw, or request correction of factual errors before any organisation-level result is published. We will not publish your organisation name, website, screenshots, metrics, or quotations without the permission specified in the participation agreement.

Would you like to review the pilot information sheet?

Regards,

[Research lead]
Nexis Research Institute
NexisHub
