# Administration Incident Response

1. Detect and record the incident without exposing sensitive data in public logs.
2. Classify severity and identify affected records or routes.
3. Contain access by disabling the smallest affected feature flag.
4. Rotate compromised secrets and revoke affected sessions or tokens.
5. Preserve audit evidence and a timeline.
6. Assess privacy, legal, research, and client-communication obligations.
7. Restore from a verified backup only when integrity is established.
8. Record root cause, corrective action, and retest evidence.

Legal review and privacy review remain pending. This procedure is an operational draft.
