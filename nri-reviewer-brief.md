# NRI AI Visibility Index Reviewer Brief

## Role

Reviewers apply the NRI annotation guide to public website and AI-assisted discovery evidence. The role is classification and documentation. It is not promotion, ranking, sales, or advocacy for a product.

## Reviewer responsibilities

- complete calibration before pilot annotation
- use the supplied labels consistently
- record direct evidence and source locations
- mark uncertainty rather than guessing
- declare conflicts of interest
- keep raw observations and participant information confidential
- preserve the original label when an adjudication occurs
- report collection failures and ambiguous cases

## Reviewer independence

Reviewers must disclose employment, ownership, advisory, investment, client, or personal relationships that could affect judgement about a sampled organisation or product.

A conflict does not always require exclusion, but it must be recorded and the research lead must decide whether reassignment is necessary.

## Time estimate for the pilot

The research lead should define the final workload after calibration. A preliminary allocation is:

- calibration: six synthetic cases
- page review: up to five pages per organisation
- discovery review: twelve declared observations per organisation
- duplicate review: at least 20 percent of records
- adjudication: only disputed or high-risk records

## Reviewer output

Each reviewed record must contain:

- reviewer identifier
- review date
- label by dimension
- evidence quote or source location
- confidence
- uncertainty note
- conflict declaration

## Quality rule

If a reviewer cannot explain a label using the evidence available in the record, the label is not ready for analysis.
