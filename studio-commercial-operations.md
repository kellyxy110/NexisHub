# NexisHub Studio Commercial Operations

## Working defaults

- Public name: NexisHub Studio
- Engagement model: paid discovery sprint, scoped build, launch support, and optional retainer
- Pricing: custom proposal after discovery
- Minimum engagement: no public price promise; internally require a paid discovery or an approved fixed brief before estimating a build
- Payment default: 50 percent at signing, 30 percent at an agreed delivery milestone, 20 percent before production handover, subject to counsel review
- Support default: launch warranty for agreed defects, followed by a separate maintenance or retainer agreement
- Change control: work outside the approved scope requires a written change request with impact on price, timing, dependencies, and acceptance

These are working commercial defaults, not signed client terms. Counsel must approve the legal language before external use.

## Required templates

### Proposal

Client context, desired outcome, recommended engagement, deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, schedule, dependencies, team, fees, payment milestones, risks, ownership, confidentiality, support, acceptance, validity period, and next step.

### Statement of work

Parties, scope, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, responsibilities, access, data handling, IP, confidentiality, fees, change control, support, termination, and signatures.

### Estimate

Work package, estimate range, confidence, assumptions, dependencies, risk category, contingency, exclusions, and approval owner. Estimates are not guarantees.

### Invoice

Entity details, client details, reference, milestone, amount, tax treatment, due date, payment method, and dispute contact. Counsel and finance must confirm the final format.

### Change request

Requested change, reason, affected deliverable, impact, revised price, revised timing, risks, decision, and signatures.

### Onboarding

Contacts, communication cadence, repositories, environments, access owners, data classification, security contacts, approvals, escalation, and project kickoff record.

### Support and incident response

Support scope, hours, severity levels, response targets, exclusions, maintenance windows, incident owner, communication route, and post-incident review.

### Closure

Acceptance record, handover materials, credentials ownership, deployment notes, monitoring, open risks, support transition, retention and deletion actions, permissions, and retrospective.
