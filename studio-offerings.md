# NexisHub Studio Initial Offerings

## 1. Product discovery sprint

Audience: organisations with an important problem but an uncertain product definition.

Deliverables: interviews, workflow review, problem statement, opportunity assessment, prioritised scope, risks, and a decision record.

Exclusions: production implementation, legal advice, guaranteed market validation, and unapproved research claims.

## 2. SaaS product design and engineering

Audience: teams building a new subscription or operational platform.

Deliverables: product architecture, interface design, application engineering, testing, deployment, and handover.

## 3. AI workflow and knowledge system implementation

Audience: organisations with a defined workflow where retrieval, document intelligence, or controlled automation may improve work.

Deliverables: risk assessment, knowledge design, integration, evaluation set, human review plan, monitoring, and rollout controls.

## 4. Existing product architecture and performance audit

Audience: teams experiencing reliability, cost, speed, maintainability, or scaling problems.

Deliverables: evidence-based audit, risk register, prioritised remediation plan, and verification method.

## 5. Website, knowledge hub, and documentation platform

Audience: organisations that need a clear public information system or maintainable internal knowledge surface.

Deliverables: information architecture, content model, accessible interface, search and metadata, publishing workflow, and handover.

## 6. Accessibility and AI visibility improvement programme

Audience: organisations improving public information quality, accessibility, machine discovery, and source clarity.

Deliverables: audit, prioritised changes, evidence and provenance recommendations, accessibility review, implementation support, and measurement plan.

Every offering requires a defined brief, audience, duration range, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and next step before proposal.
