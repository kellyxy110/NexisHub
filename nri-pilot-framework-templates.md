# NRI Pilot Framework Templates

## Pilot proposal

Title, purpose, research questions, sample, inclusion criteria, exclusions, data basis, collection method, risks, retention, withdrawal, correction, publication treatment, reviewers, timeline, and approval gates.

## Participant information sheet

Study purpose, what will happen, public and optional data, risks, benefits, confidentiality, retention, withdrawal, correction, publication choices, contact owner, and questions.

## Consent form

Organisation name, representative, selected permissions, data categories, publication permissions, attribution choice, withdrawal route, correction route, signature, date, and version of information sheet.

## Pilot checklist

- [ ] Protocol version approved
- [ ] Legal or ethics review completed where required
- [ ] Research lead named
- [ ] Reviewers calibrated
- [ ] Contact owner confirmed
- [ ] Information sheet delivered
- [ ] Consent recorded
- [ ] Collection log opened
- [ ] Data minimisation confirmed
- [ ] Withdrawal and correction route tested

## Data collection record

Organisation ID, URL, collection date, collector, tool and version, query or task, source location, observation, evidence, confidence, uncertainty, and retention class.

## Analysis template

Sample description, missing data, reviewer agreement, adjudication, distributions, subgroup analysis, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty, alternative explanations, and limitations.

## Lessons learned report

What was expected, what occurred, recruitment lessons, method issues, reviewer issues, participant feedback, ethical issues, changes required, and what would change the next pilot.

## Pilot completion report

Final sample, consent status, collection completion, deviations, data disposition, analysis status, corrections, publication permissions, open risks, and recommendation to proceed or stop.
