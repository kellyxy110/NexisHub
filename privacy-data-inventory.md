# Privacy Data Inventory (Draft)

**Status:** Internal draft. Privacy policy and legal review pending.

| Data category | Current surface | Purpose | Sensitivity | Retention decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contact and Studio enquiry fields | Webhook form routes | Respond to enquiries | Business/contact data | Founder approval and legal review required |
| Newsletter email | Subscription route | Deliver requested updates | Personal data | Retention and unsubscribe process required |
| Consent preferences | Browser controls | Respect analytics preferences | Preference data | Minimise and document expiry |
| Analytics event payloads | Optional analytics webhook | Understand product usage | Potentially identifiable metadata | Disabled unless consent and provider review exist |
| Publication metadata | File-backed registry | Public research discovery | Public/editorial data | Version and correction history |
| Reviewer records | Not implemented | Future review workflow | Confidential professional data | Must remain private and gated |
| Pilot and consent records | Not implemented | Future research operations | Sensitive research data | Must remain disabled until legal/ethics review |
| Studio commercial records | Not implemented | Future delivery operations | Confidential business data | Access-controlled retention required |

No participant, reviewer, client permission, legal, or financial records are seeded by this repository.
