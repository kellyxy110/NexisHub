# Citation and Download Guide

The first NRI working paper exposes citation downloads at:

- `/research/publications/ai-visibility-index/citation?format=json`
- `/research/publications/ai-visibility-index/citation?format=bibtex`
- `/research/publications/ai-visibility-index/citation?format=apa`

These are citation records, not proof of peer review or DOI registration. The DOI field remains null until a DOI is genuinely registered.
