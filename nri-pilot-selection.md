## Selection rules

The pilot coordinator should add organisations only when the research lead confirms:

1. The public website is reachable and within scope.
2. The organisation fits a declared sector and region category.
3. The invitation recipient is an appropriate representative.
4. The data basis, retention period, correction route, and publication treatment are disclosed.
5. Any relationship with NexisHub is recorded as a potential conflict.

## Invitation gate

Invitations may be sent only after a named research lead approves the final message and a valid contact channel is recorded. An invitation is not consent. Acceptance must be captured separately.
