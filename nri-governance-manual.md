# Nexis Research Institute Governance Manual

## Maturity stage

NRI is currently a Stage 1 founder-led institute.

1. Founder-led institute: charter, programme roadmap, and publication system are established; appointments are pending.
2. Collaborative institute: named reviewers and collaborators accept defined roles.
3. Research fellows: recurring projects have accountable researchers and maintained outputs.
4. International institute: durable partnerships, independent review, and global research programmes are established.

This maturity description is not an accreditation claim.

## Research integrity statement

NRI separates observation, interpretation, assumption, and recommendation. It reports uncertainty, preserves inconvenient results, records changes, and corrects material errors. It does not use research language to disguise product marketing.

## Ethics statement

Research must minimise data, use an appropriate legal and ethical basis, protect participant dignity, disclose conflicts, provide withdrawal and correction routes, and avoid publishing identifiable information without permission.

## Conflict of interest policy

Every researcher, reviewer, collaborator, and study owner must disclose employment, ownership, client, investment, advisory, personal, or product relationships that could affect judgement. The research lead records mitigation or reassignment.

## Publication policy

The lifecycle is Draft, Internal Technical Review, Editorial Review, External Review where applicable, Revision, Publication, and Version History. Publication status must never imply peer review unless the review record says so.

## Peer review policy

Reviewers assess evidence, method, clarity, limitations, reproducibility, and ethics. They do not approve commercial claims. Names, roles, conflicts, review dates, requested changes, and responses are recorded when publication permissions allow.

## Open data policy

Data may be released only when ownership, privacy, consent, licensing, security, and re-identification risks have been assessed. Restricted data must be described without being exposed.

## Correction and retraction policy

Minor factual errors receive a dated correction note. Material errors receive a prominent correction and version update. Unreliable or unethical work may be retracted with the reason preserved. The original record is never silently replaced.

## Citation policy

Sources must be identifiable and relevant. NRI cites primary material where practical, distinguishes source claims from NRI findings, and records software, datasets, models, versions, and access dates when applicable.

## AI usage policy

AI tools may assist drafting, coding, transcription, or analysis only when a responsible human verifies the result. Sensitive or restricted material must not be entered into an unapproved system. Material AI assistance and verification methods should be disclosed.

## Research versioning

Use semantic publication states where appropriate: v0.1 Draft, v0.9 Review, v1.0 Published, and later revisions. Every version records date, status, authorship, review level, changes, and known limitations.

## Copyright and publication licence

Copyright remains with the stated rights holder unless a separate licence says otherwise. Each publication must state its reuse terms. No Creative Commons or open-source licence should be implied before ownership and approval are confirmed.
