# NexisHub Legal Review Packet

## Purpose

This packet prepares the NexisHub website and Studio materials for review by qualified legal counsel. It is not legal approval and must not be published as evidence that counsel has approved the documents.

## Documents requiring review

- `website/src/app/legal/privacy/page.tsx`
- `website/src/app/legal/terms/page.tsx`
- `website/src/app/legal/cookies/page.tsx`
- `website/src/components/legal-document.tsx`
- `website/src/app/api/contact/route.ts`
- `website/src/app/api/subscribe/route.ts`
- `website/src/app/api/events/route.ts`
- Studio enquiry fields and webhook processing
- NRI research data collection and publication plans

## Facts counsel must confirm

- Legal entity name and registration jurisdiction
- Registered or service address
- Governing law and dispute venue
- Privacy controller and official privacy contact
- Data processing locations and vendors
- Form, email, analytics, hosting, monitoring, and payment providers
- Retention periods and deletion process
- User rights and response process
- Cookie and local-storage classification
- International transfer disclosures
- Studio engagement terms, intellectual property, confidentiality, warranties, liability, and support
- NRI research ethics, consent, data use, and publication correction process

## Review questions

1. Does the Privacy Policy accurately describe every production data flow?
2. Does the Cookie Policy match the actual consent and analytics implementation?
3. Do the Terms cover the company website, Studio enquiries, external products, intellectual property, links, availability, liability, and applicable law?
4. Are the legal documents suitable for the jurisdictions where NexisHub operates?
5. Are the Studio enquiry fields proportionate and properly protected?
6. Does the NRI protocol require additional consent, review, or data protection controls?
