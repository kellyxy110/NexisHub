# Data Retention Policy Draft

**Status:** Draft. Legal and privacy policy review pending.

Retain only the minimum data needed for the stated purpose. Define retention periods for enquiry delivery, newsletter subscriptions, analytics events, publication versions, reviewer records, pilot records, consent records, Studio records, and audit events before enabling those modules.

Withdrawal, deletion, export, legal hold, correction, and retraction procedures must be defined for each category. Historical research publication records may require preservation for citation integrity, subject to legal and ethics review.

No retention period in this draft should be treated as an approved legal requirement.
