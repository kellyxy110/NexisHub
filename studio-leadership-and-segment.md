# NexisHub Studio Leadership and Target Segment Record

## Founder authorization

The founder authorizes the following operating model for planning. Names and contractual appointments remain pending explicit acceptance by the people involved.

## Roles

- Studio lead: accountable for positioning, proposals, delivery quality, and escalation. Name pending.
- Product lead: accountable for discovery, scope, prioritisation, and client decisions. Name pending.
- Technical lead: accountable for architecture, engineering quality, security, and release readiness. Name pending.
- Design lead: accountable for interaction, accessibility, design system, and validation. Name pending.
- Delivery coordinator: accountable for schedule, risks, decisions, and handover. Name pending.

## Initial target segment

Primary: established African organisations and funded startups in education, healthcare, professional services, technology, and public-interest work that have a meaningful workflow or product problem and an accountable decision maker.

Good fit: a defined user group, access to subject matter experts, willingness to fund discovery, realistic ownership expectations, and a need for maintainable software rather than a one-off marketing page.

Poor fit: projects seeking unpaid speculative work, guaranteed growth, unbounded scope, private data without a lawful basis, or a public case study without permission.

## Decision still required
