# Editorial Maintenance Automation

Run from `website/`:

```text
node scripts/editorial-maintenance.mjs
```

The check confirms the post registry, NRI programme registry, publication registry, sitemap, and robots files exist; confirms at least one published editorial record; and confirms the 30-programme registry remains populated.

This is a source-integrity check, not a factual review. Human source review, product verification, legal review, and founder approval remain separate tasks.
