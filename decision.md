# NexisHub Studio Decision Record

## Decision

NexisHub Studio will be established as the product engineering and digital innovation arm of NexisHub.

Studio will help external organisations discover, design, engineer, launch, improve, and maintain digital products. It is not positioned as a traditional web design agency, freelancer marketplace, or code-only delivery team.

## Positioning

NexisHub Studio is a product engineering studio for intelligent software and scalable digital platforms.

The working positioning is:

> We design, architect, build, launch, optimise, and support digital products.

The Studio sells engineering capability and long-term product outcomes. It must not promise outcomes that have not been scoped, measured, or approved.

## Working tagline

The initial working tagline is:

> Engineering Intelligent Products.

Other proposed taglines remain options for a later brand decision. Public materials should use one primary tagline at a time.

## Vision

NexisHub Studio aims to become an Africa-leading product engineering studio for intelligent software, AI-powered solutions, and scalable digital platforms.

This is a long-term ambition and must not be presented as an established market position without supporting evidence.

## Mission

Studio exists to help organisations transform ambitious ideas into reliable, scalable, accessible, and well-engineered software products that create measurable business value.

## Core product standard

Every Studio product should be:

- purpose-driven
- human-centred
- AI-ready where appropriate
- search-visible
- accessible
- scalable to its defined demand
- secure for its risk profile
- maintainable by its intended team
- informed by data and user evidence
- documented well enough to operate after launch

Future-proof is treated as a design aspiration, not a guarantee. Every project must state its assumptions, expected lifespan, constraints, and maintenance plan.

## Services

Studio may provide the following services:

1. Product discovery: business analysis, stakeholder interviews, research, competitor review, user research, and feature prioritisation.
2. Product design: information architecture, user flows, wireframes, interface design, design systems, prototypes, and accessibility review.
3. Product engineering: full-stack applications, SaaS platforms, APIs, cloud systems, data systems, and internal enterprise software.
4. Artificial intelligence: agents, assistants, RAG systems, semantic search, document intelligence, workflow automation, recommendations, and evaluation systems.
5. Product optimisation: performance, accessibility, security, architecture, refactoring, scalability, SEO, and AI visibility improvements.

The service catalogue is a capability map. A specific engagement must define scope, deliverables, dependencies, exclusions, acceptance criteria, ownership, and support terms.

## Target organisations

Studio may serve startups, established businesses, educational institutions, healthcare organisations, government agencies, NGOs, and enterprises.

Industry experience may include education, healthcare, real estate, construction, government, finance, retail, technology, manufacturing, agriculture, media, professional services, religious organisations, and hospitality.

The Studio must not imply specialist regulatory competence in healthcare, finance, government, or other regulated sectors until the required expertise and review arrangements are confirmed.

## Delivery method

The default delivery method has eight phases:

1. Discovery
2. Research
3. Strategy
4. Design
5. Engineering
6. Quality assurance
7. Launch
8. Growth and maintenance

Phases may overlap, but the decision record, research evidence, design approval, engineering acceptance, and launch readiness must remain traceable.

## Technology principles

Studio projects should prefer:

- open-source technologies where they are suitable
- scalable architecture proportional to actual demand
- cloud-native deployment where justified
- reusable components and shared design systems
- API-first boundaries for integrations
- security and privacy by design
- performance by default
- automated and manual testing
- documentation before handoff

The preferred implementation stack may include Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, Supabase, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, AI integrations, and modern cloud infrastructure. The stack is a default, not a promise. Each engagement should select technology based on product requirements, team capability, risk, budget, and maintenance needs.

## Relationship with NexisHub

NexisHub is the parent company responsible for proprietary products, research, shared infrastructure, and company direction. NexisHub Studio extends these capabilities to external organisations.

Studio may reuse proven components, practices, and infrastructure patterns from SiteNexis, TeachNexis, EventNexis, CareBridge, LogicLand, and NRI research. Client work must remain appropriately isolated. Client data, source code, credentials, research, and intellectual property must not be reused across projects without written permission.

The Studio must not claim that every client project uses the same internal infrastructure. Public descriptions must distinguish shared engineering principles from project-specific implementation.

## Portfolio and case studies

Each case study should use this structure:

- business challenge
- research and discovery
- strategy
- design
- technology
- development
- launch
- measurable business impact
- lessons learned
- approved client testimonial

No case study may publish client names, logos, screenshots, metrics, testimonials, or technical details without approval and appropriate rights.

## Client products and NexisHub products

Studio portfolio material must distinguish:

- NexisHub proprietary products
- Studio client products
- internal prototypes
- conceptual examples

The Studio must not imply that a client product is owned by NexisHub or that a NexisHub product was built for a client.

## Commercial and legal boundaries

The following require a separate approved agreement or founder decision:

- pricing and engagement models
- minimum project size
- payment milestones
- intellectual property ownership
- support and service-level commitments
- data processing terms
- confidentiality terms
- subcontracting
- warranties and liability
- regulated-sector responsibilities
- client references and portfolio permissions

Do not publish invented client results, client logos, named collaborators, financial claims, or performance guarantees.

## Research relationship

Nexis Research Institute may investigate engineering methods, AI systems, product development, education technology, healthcare informatics, and related topics that inform Studio practice.

NRI research must remain independent. Studio engagements must not be presented as neutral research unless they satisfy the NRI research standard and receive the required review.
